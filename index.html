Our roots

WE DIDN'T START WITH NOTEBOOKS. We started with paper.

I have been around paper for as long as I can remember.

In 1997, my father began trading paper in Indore. I grew up around reams, printing presses, and the people who worked with them.

Over time, paper became more than our family business. It became something I genuinely cared about.

In 2020, we decided to take that experience beyond paper trading and create something of our own. Penkraft was born from that thought — turning a material we had spent years understanding into something people could experience for themselves.

We care about the details that make a notebook feel right — the paper, the construction, the design, and everything in between.

I still design every Penkraft cover myself. For me, it has never been just about making notebooks.

It is about making something I would want to pick up, open, and write on.

And we are only getting started.

Aditya Gupta inFounder, House of Penkraft
